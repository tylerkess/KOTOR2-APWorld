# Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II

## What changes

Peragus, the Harbinger and Telos play as usual. Once the Ebon Hawk is yours, every planet on the galaxy map stays
greyed out until its coordinates arrive: Nar Shaddaa, Dxun (which leads on to Onderon), Dantooine, Korriban and, with
the M4-78 Enhancement Project, M4-78. The endgame opens the usual way once you have found all four hidden masters:
Nar Shaddaa, Onderon, Dantooine and Korriban (M4-78 instead of Korriban for Lonna Vash with M4-78EP). With shuffled
Jedi Masters, the four masters are items and the meeting on Dantooine waits for them instead.

Plays on the vanilla game, TSLRCM, or TSLRCM with the M4-78 Enhancement Project (the `content` option). You win by defeating Kreia on Malachor V.

## Checks

- Entering an area for the first time.
- Completing a quest, whatever the outcome (light side, dark side and faction endings all count).

Side quests, optional areas and everything from the masters' meeting on only hold filler: a choice can close a side
quest, and the endgame never lets you go back.

## Items

Planet coordinates and Jedi Masters (progression), and filler that follows your level when it arrives:
- **Gear** (Weapon, Armor, Equipment, Upgrade, Lightsaber Part, Droid Gear): picked from the game's own loot tables,
  in the range the game would drop at your level, sometimes a few tiers better.
- **Lightsaber**: a random single, short or double-bladed saber in a random colour, sometimes a unique one.
- **Credits** (150 per level) and **Experience** (a quarter of what your level needs), counted from the level your
  experience has earned, levelled up or not.
- Medpacs, stimulants, components, chemicals, computer and security spikes.

Received items show as floating text over your character.
